1. Introduction

In today’s academic environment, students often face difficulty in managing their study schedules and completing tasks on time. Traditional planning methods such as notebooks and paper schedules are difficult to maintain and update regularly.

The Smart Study Planner is designed to solve this problem by providing a digital platform for managing study activities. The application helps students organize tasks, set priorities, and monitor progress in a simple and efficient manner.

The project aims to increase productivity and reduce stress by helping students plan their studies systematically.

2. Objectives

The main objectives of the Smart Study Planner are:

To help students manage study schedules efficiently.
To organize subjects and tasks systematically.
To improve time management and productivity.
To provide reminders for important tasks.
To track completed and pending work.
To create a simple and user-friendly interface.
3. Problem Statement

Many students struggle with managing multiple subjects, assignments, and examination schedules. Manual planning methods are often inefficient and difficult to maintain.

There is a need for a smart system that can help students:

Plan their daily study activities.
Track progress regularly.
Manage time effectively.
Reduce workload confusion.

The Smart Study Planner addresses these issues through automation and digital task management.
